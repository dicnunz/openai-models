Scope & method

Includes publicly documented OpenAI generative language-model families and materially distinct public variants. Dated API snapshots and simple aliases are collapsed into their families. Embedding, moderation, image/video-only, transcription-only and TTS-only models are excluded.

Role rows describe public positioning, not unpublished checkpoint ancestry. Capability marks and technical details use public documentation; unknowns stay unknown. Each selected model links to its primary source in the comparison.
